# Below Quota — Support

**Below Quota** is a dark dungeon crawler for iPhone: step through the portal, light your way with a torch, bring the loot back to the lair and meet the quota.

## Contact

Questions, bug reports or ideas: **krasovskyi.yevhenii@gmail.com**. Please include your device model and iOS version, and describe what happened.

## Frequently asked questions

**How do I start over?**
Main menu → New Game. This erases the current run, your card deck and your coins.

**How do I change the language?**
Settings → Language. The Game supports English and Russian.

**How do I turn off personalized ads?**
Settings → Ads → Non-personalized. Ads will still be shown, but not personalized. You can also deny tracking in iOS Settings → Privacy & Security → Tracking.

**Can I play without ads?**
Not at the moment: ads keep the Game free.

**Where is my progress stored? Can it be deleted?**
Only on your device. Deleting the Game deletes your progress. We keep no copy of it.
