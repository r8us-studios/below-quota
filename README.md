# Below Quota — Privacy Policy

**Effective date:** November 1, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how the mobile game **Below Quota** (the "Game") handles information. The Game is developed and published by [NAME] ("we", "us").

## Summary

- We do not ask for your name, email address or any account.
- Your progress and settings are stored only on your device.
- The Game shows ads through Unity LevelPlay and Unity Ads. These partners collect device and advertising data to deliver ads. Personalized ads and tracking happen only with your permission.

## 1. Data stored on your device

The Game saves your progress (current run, card deck, coins), your settings (volume, language) and your advertising choice on your device. This data is not sent to us. Deleting the Game removes it.

## 2. Data we collect

We do not collect, receive or store personal data ourselves. The Game has no accounts, no chat, and no analytics or crash reporting of our own.

## 3. Advertising

The Game is free and shows ads. Ads are served by **Unity LevelPlay** (ad mediation operated by ironSource Mobile Ltd.) and **Unity Ads**, both part of Unity Technologies ("Unity"). When the Game requests and shows ads, Unity's software in the Game may collect:

- device identifiers: the Identifier for Vendors (IDFV) and, only if you allow tracking, the Advertising Identifier (IDFA);
- your IP address and the approximate (country-level) location derived from it;
- device and app information, such as device model, operating system version, language, screen size, connection type and app version;
- advertising data, such as which ads were shown, tapped or closed;
- performance data, such as how long the ad software takes to start.

Unity uses this data to deliver and measure ads, limit how often you see the same ad, prevent fraud and, if you have agreed, show personalized ads. Unity processes this data as an independent controller under its own privacy policy:
[Unity Game Player and App User Privacy Policy](https://unity.com/legal/game-player-and-app-user-privacy-policy).

## 4. Your choices

- **Consent (EEA, United Kingdom, Switzerland).** On first launch the Game asks whether ads may be personalized. If you decline, you still see ads, but they are not personalized.
- **App Tracking Transparency.** If personalized ads are on, iOS asks whether the Game may track you. You can change this at any time in iOS Settings → Privacy & Security → Tracking.
- **In-game setting.** Settings → Ads → Personalized or Non-personalized. You can change your choice at any time.
- **United States residents.** Choosing Non-personalized also tells our ad partners not to sell or share your personal information (an opt-out under the CCPA/CPRA and similar state laws).

## 5. Legal bases (EEA and United Kingdom)

Personalized advertising is based on your consent (Article 6(1)(a) GDPR), which you can withdraw at any time in the Game's Settings. Non-personalized advertising, which keeps the Game free, is based on our legitimate interest (Article 6(1)(f) GDPR).

## 6. Children

The Game is not directed at children under 13, or under the minimum age set by the law of your country. We do not knowingly collect children's personal data. If you believe a child has used the Game and our ad partners collected their data, contact us and we will help.

## 7. Data retention and security

We keep no personal data on our side. Data on your device stays there until you delete the Game. Unity keeps the data it collects according to its privacy policy.

## 8. Your rights

Depending on where you live, you may have the right to access, correct or delete your personal data, to object to its processing, to withdraw consent and to lodge a complaint with a data protection authority (in Poland: the President of the Personal Data Protection Office, UODO). Because we hold no personal data, requests about advertising data are best sent to Unity using the link above. If you write to us, we will help.

## 9. Changes

If this policy changes, we will update this page and its effective date.
